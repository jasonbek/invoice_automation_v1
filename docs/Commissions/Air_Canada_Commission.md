# Air Canada — Time of Ticketing Commission (Lookup Table)

Point of Sale: Canada. First ticketing/outbound: July 1, 2026. Last ticketing: n/a.

This file is a structured lookup table distilled from the official Confidential Incentive
Agreement. Follow the 4 steps in order for every ticket/passenger — do not skip a step or
infer a rate from memory.

## Carrier definitions

- **Air Canada Carriers**: Air Canada, Air Canada Rouge, Air Canada Express (any carrier
  operating under those brands via capacity purchase/wet lease), and The Landline Company
  Inc. (ground transport).
- **Transatlantic Joint Venture (JV) Carriers**: Air Canada Carriers + Lufthansa, Austrian
  Airlines, Swiss International Air Lines, Brussels Airlines, Edelweiss Air, Discover
  Airlines, United Airlines, and their wet-lease operators.
- **China JV Carriers**: Air Canada Carriers + Air China.
- **Canada-domestic additional permitted carriers** (count toward "Online" only for a
  domestic feeder segment, and only when at least one Canadian segment is marketed AND
  operated by an Air Canada Carrier / that region's JV carrier): Air Creebec, Bearskin,
  Canadian North, Central Mountain Air, PAL Airlines, Pascan Aviation.

## Commission applies to / does NOT apply to (all regions)

**Applies to**: original tickets and ticket exchanges; base fare, surcharge, stopover
charges, and elements shown in the fare box/fare calculation box; published fares sold as
one-way, round-trip, open-jaw, side-trip, end-on-end.

**Does NOT apply to (0% / do not calculate commission)**: change fees, seat selection
fees, meals, upgrades, and other optional product/service fees; all net fares including
Leisure/Group net fares and IT/BT (no-fare ticketing); Small/Medium Business tickets with a
PN# or other account number; corporate contract tickets (AC or JV corporate contract
number, or any other corporate contract number, in the tour code box — even with no
corporate discount applied); Aeroplan redemption tickets; industry reduced rate tickets;
Air Canada Vacations air-only tickets; bereavement travel; any other discount program
fares; taxes/surcharges/fees entered in the tax boxes; Round-the-World (RTW) fares; infant
fares not occupying a seat; tickets issued via the Air Canada web link; Group travel
(except where a specific "Group Travel" rate is listed in a region's table below).

Refunds recall the commission. Split ticketing (issuing two one-ways instead of one
round-trip) is not permitted.

## STEP 2 — Online vs Interline

- **Online** = the region-qualifying segment(s) are marketed AND operated by that region's
  required carrier(s) (see each region's "Plating / Permitted carriers" line below), plus
  any of that region's specifically-listed feeder/codeshare exceptions.
- **Interline** = the region-qualifying segment(s) are still marketed+operated by the
  required carrier, but up to 2 segments per direction are on OTHER, non-JV airlines. This
  is a MIX of Air Canada (or its JV) with other airlines — not "all Air Canada."
- **Regions 1 (North America) and 2 (Sun) have NO interline rate** — every segment on the
  ticket must be an Air Canada Carrier (or the Canada-domestic additional permitted
  carriers) for the ticket to be commissionable at all under those tables.

below. Never infer the row from the cabin name alone — different codes in the same cabin
can carry different rates (e.g. a Business-cabin fare filed under a "BA…" fare basis is
Economy Basic, 0%, not the Business rate).

## STEP 4 — Mixed booking classes on one ticket

- **Regions 1–2 (North America / Sun)**: the LOWEST rate across the WHOLE ticket applies.
- **Regions 3–7 (all international regions)**: the LOWEST rate among the INTERNATIONAL
  segments only applies to the whole ticket. Domestic "feeder" legs do NOT downgrade the
  international commission.

## Booking/ticketing operational notes

- **Tour Code ACTOT**: must be entered in the GDS tour code box at time of ticketing for
  every region except North America and Sun. Missing or incorrect ACTOT (where required) ->
  minimum $50/ticket ticketing error fee, and the commission may be recalled.
- **Stopover**: Transatlantic itineraries with interline segments — stopovers permitted per
  fare rule, or if end-on-end combination is permitted, max 24 hours. Other regions —
  stopovers per fare rules.
- **Booking channels**: NDC-certified channels (incl. AC Connex) and GDS. On GDS, enter the
  incentive amount in the commission box at time of ticketing; on NDC channels the rate is
  applied automatically and paid via BSP.
